About INDKAN World Wide Exim

Headquartered in the modern commercial hub of New Town, Kolkata, INDKAN World Wide Exim Pvt. Ltd. connects international apparel importers with skilled Indian textile clusters. Bengal and eastern India are world-famous for cotton weaving, muslin, and fine silks. We bring these heritage garments to global retail shelves with consistent sizing, quality stitching, and reliable delivery timelines.

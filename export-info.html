Export Information & Shipping

We make international apparel procurement straightforward and transparent. Below is our step-by-step export workflow, global destinations, payment options, and essential customs documentation.

Our 7-Step Export Process

From your initial inquiry to final port delivery, here is how an order works

1

Enquiry & Quotation

You send your product requirements, target quantities, and destination port. We provide a detailed proforma quotation in Indian Rupees (₹) with FOB Kolkata or CIF rates.

2

Sample Approval

We dispatch fabric swatches and pre-production counter-samples by international courier (DHL/FedEx) for your fit, color, and fabric verification.

3

Order Confirmation

Once you approve the sample, a formal purchase contract is signed. The buyer opens an Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C) or remits the agreed 30% advance deposit.

4

Production & Stitching

Bulk production begins at our partner manufacturing facilities. Fabric is woven, dyed, cut, stitched, and finished as per the signed tech-pack specifications.

5

Quality Check & Packing

Each garment is individually checked for stitch tension, measurements, and finishing. Garments are polybagged with barcode tags and packed into 7-ply export cartons.

6

Customs Clearance & Shipping

Goods are moved to Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port, Kolkata (or Kolkata Air Cargo). Indian export customs clearance is processed and cartons are loaded into container vessels.

7

Delivery & Documents Handover

Shipping Bill of Lading (BL), invoice, and Certificate of Origin are released through the banking channel or courier, enabling smooth clearance at your local port.

Countries We Ship To

We regularly ship commercial apparel consignments to international buyers across:

  • North America: United States, Canada
  • Europe & UK: United Kingdom, Germany, France, Netherlands, Italy, Spain
  • Middle East & GCC: United Arab Emirates (Dubai, Jebel Ali), Saudi Arabia, Oman
  • Asia-Pacific: Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Malaysia
  • Africa: South Africa, Kenya, Mauritius

Payment Terms

To ensure complete safety and compliance under Indian foreign exchange (RBI) rules:

  • Letter of Credit (L/C): 100% Irrevocable Letter of Credit at sight through prime international banks.
  • Telegraphic Transfer (T/T): 30% advance on order confirmation, balance 70% payable against scanned copy of shipping Bill of Lading.
  • Currency: Quoted in Indian Rupees (₹) with USD / EUR / GBP exchange conversions on proforma invoices.

Export Documents Provided

Complete paperwork provided with every commercial shipment

DGFT IEC

Importer-Exporter Code issued by Ministry of Commerce, Govt. of India.

GST Export Invoice

Official tax invoice with itemized HS codes, unit prices, and quantities.

Packing List

Gross and net weights, carton count, and itemized piece breakdown per box.

Certificate of Origin

Issued by the authorized Indian Chamber of Commerce for preferential customs duties.
